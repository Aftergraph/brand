# STEWARD Dark Brandbook

Status: product-extension guidance. The Aftergraph master brand remains canonical.

## Night palette

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| night_background | #0B1120 | primary dark canvas |
| night_text | #F4F7FF | primary text |
| night_border | #2A3A5C | quiet structural boundaries |
| steward_copper | #C2703D | identity, custody, deliberate attention |
| pine_teal | #23615E | evidence and verification progress |
| moss | #3E7C59 | settled success when backed by evidence |

Do not substitute Aftergraph master-brand tokens in a way that makes a STEWARD product surface look like a different canonical owner.

## Surfaces

1. Keep the Bounded Node readable at 24 px and above.
2. Use night_background as the base, not pure black.
3. Use night_border for static structure. Copper and teal are signals, not decoration.
4. Reserve success treatment for states backed by real evidence.
5. Approval, blocked, failed, and stale states require text labels in addition to color.

## Presence

A dark surface may use the 3D keeper or Lottie presence signal. Motion must follow `motion/presence-contract.json`. Reduced-motion users receive the same semantic state with no continuous movement.

## Copy

Use short, literal state language. Prefer "Verification in progress" over theatrical language. Prefer "Evidence unavailable" over a confidence-shaped visual substitute.

## Claims

A polished dark UI does not upgrade system maturity. Product identity never inherits authority, execution, conformance, or scientific claims from another Aftergraph repository.
