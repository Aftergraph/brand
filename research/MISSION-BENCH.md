# MISSION-Bench Visual Kit

MISSION-Bench is an Aftergraph Research benchmark family, not an independent corporate brand.

Canonical failure-injection names used by this kit are taken from current research artifacts: `tool_timeout`, `bad_output`, `stale_state`, `revocation`, `context_loss`, `budget_exhaustion`, `partial_execution`, `environment_change`, `verifier_failure`, and `model_failure`.

Every results visual requires a visible execution classification. Existing 800-run ablation results are labelled `CONTROLLED SIMULATION`; they must not be presented as independent or live-provider evidence.

Generated assets include the benchmark glyph, ten failure glyphs, eight-step ablation ladder, result card template, run receipt, reproducibility card, and social card source.
