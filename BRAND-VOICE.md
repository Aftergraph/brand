# Aftergraph Brand Voice

## Position

Aftergraph builds infrastructure and open research for verifiable intelligent systems. The product is not a personality, a magic model, or generic automation. It is the institution layer that makes long-running machine work bounded, inspectable, recoverable, and independently verifiable.

## Primary line

**Infrastructure for verifiable intelligent systems.**

## Supporting lines

- Govern the mission. Verify the outcome.
- From model output to accountable execution.
- Authority in. Evidence out.
- Intelligence that can show its work.

## Voice rules

- Lead with the system, evidence, or user outcome.
- Name maturity and evidence class precisely.
- Prefer verbs: govern, constrain, verify, attest, replay, recover.
- Avoid superlatives, sentience language, cosmic AI imagery, and certainty beyond evidence.
- Distinguish deterministic simulation, live-provider validation, independent reproduction, and production operation.

## One-sentence descriptions

- **Aftergraph:** Infrastructure and open research for verifiable intelligent systems.
- **Work Intelligence:** A human control surface for durable work, evidence, approvals, and verified outcomes.
- **Docs:** Specifications, implementation guides, evidence models, and operating contracts for the Aftergraph system.
- **Sentinel:** Exact-state release verification with a fail-closed ship verdict.
- **Trust Gateway:** Runtime enforcement for authority, policy, budgets, and tamper-evident audit.
- **WORKS:** Durable execution infrastructure for long-running governed missions.
