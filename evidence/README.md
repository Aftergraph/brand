# Evidence Visual Language

Evidence assets classify evidence; they are not seals of commercial certification.

Required adjacent metadata: classification, source, exact revision, verifier or study, timestamp, and scope. `verified` without those fields is invalid public usage.

The generated family covers exact-head, source-pinned, reproducible, controlled-simulation, live-runtime, independent-reproduction, conformance-tested, incomplete, stale, superseded, and revoked evidence states plus E0–E6 research classifications.
