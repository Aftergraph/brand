# Aftergraph Motion System

Motion communicates state and provenance. It does not simulate intelligence or decorate empty space.

## Six-second brand ident

1. **0.0–1.2 s — State:** isolated evidence nodes appear on institution black.
2. **1.2–2.4 s — Relation:** thin evidence-white edges resolve a graph.
3. **2.4–3.4 s — Boundary:** control-cyan hexagonal perimeter closes around the graph.
4. **3.4–4.5 s — Authority:** authority-violet signal traverses one approved edge only.
5. **4.5–5.3 s — Verification:** central kernel resolves; evidence-teal receipt pulse.
6. **5.3–6.0 s — Lockup:** Aftergraph wordmark and “Authority in. Evidence out.”

No camera shake, particles, lens flare, cyberpunk tunnels, humanoid robots, brains, or floating code.
